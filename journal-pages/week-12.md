## Project Statement 

**Whispers of care**

The Whispers of care is a tactile 3D visualisation that transforms the often invisible pulse of our community into an physical hand made paper sculpture. The work centers on the micro acts of care small everyday gestures such as cooking for someone, sharing resources or helping a neighbour water their plants. These actions are socially essential and yet they  frequently go unnoticed or unrecorded.

Set within the speculative future the project imagines a world where cities have introduced the care minutes a civic system that will recognise and track the small acts as a measurable contribution. In the scenario care becomes a valued resource that will shape how the communities would function and support each other. And by framing kindness as data the work invites the viewers to question what will be gained and what will be lost when human generosity becomes something quantified.   

The visualisation is built from the qualitative data gatherers from participants who have described their own acts of care. Each of the leaves in the plant is built directly from this dataset, the height of the stem reflects the time spent, the colour communicates the emotional tone, and the leaf patterns show the type of care offered. These patterns form a core visual language of the plant translating lived experiences into an symbolic ecosystem.

Constructed from layered, hand cut paper the this work intentionally departs from the digital dashboards. Rooted in the principles of data humanism and the slow, embodied representation, it prioritises emotional resonance over the efficiency. And instead of flattening care into numbers, the plant invites viewers to feel the texture, fragility and the presence.

Visitors are  invited to contribute by writing down their own acts of care on paper raindrops or sun, which symbolically is nourishing the plant. Through this gesture, whispers of care are encouraging people  to slow down, and notice the subtle forms of care that sustain daily life and recognise their own role within a caring community.
